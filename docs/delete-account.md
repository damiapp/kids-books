# Delete your Peekadoo account

You can delete your Peekadoo account and the data linked to it at any
time.

## In the app

1. Open the menu (☰) and choose **For grown-ups**.
2. Answer the grown-up check (type the number shown in words).
3. Tap **Delete account** at the bottom, then confirm.

The account is deleted straight away. If you signed in a while ago, the
app asks for your password once more first — that's a security check,
not a second step.

## Without the app

If you no longer have the app installed, email
**[CONTACT EMAIL — to be filled in before publishing]** from the address
you signed up with, with the subject "Delete my Peekadoo account". We
delete the account within 30 days and reply to confirm.

## What is deleted

- **Your account:** the email address and password used to sign in.
- **Learning progress saved on the phone:** lessons finished, streaks,
  and the words the child found hard. When you delete from inside the
  app, these are removed from that phone immediately. They are only ever
  stored on the phone itself, so uninstalling the app removes them too.

## What is kept

Nothing linked to you. Peekadoo does not keep a copy of a deleted
account. If you subscribed to All Access, the purchase record is held
by Google Play, not by us — cancelling or refunding it is done in the
Google Play app under **Payments & subscriptions**.
