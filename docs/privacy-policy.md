# Peekadoo privacy policy

*Effective [DATE — fill in before publishing]*

Peekadoo is a learning app for young children, made by
**[DEVELOPER NAME — fill in before publishing]**. This page explains what
the app collects, why, and what you can do about it.

## The short version

- A grown-up signs up with an **email address**. That's the only personal
  information Peekadoo asks for.
- A child's **learning progress stays on the phone**. It is never sent to
  us.
- There are **no ads, no analytics and no tracking**. We don't use the
  advertising ID, and we don't sell or share anyone's data.
- If the app **crashes**, a crash report is sent so we can fix it. It
  says what went wrong, not who was using the app, and a grown-up can
  switch it off.
- You can **delete the account at any time**, from inside the app or from
  our [account deletion page](delete-account).

## What we collect, and why

**The account.** A parent or carer creates an account with an email
address and a password, so the app knows who's signed in. This is handled
by Google's Firebase Authentication service. We never see or store the
password itself — Firebase keeps only a secure scrambled form of it.
Firebase also processes the device's IP address when you sign in, to
protect accounts from abuse.

**Learning progress.** Which lessons have been finished, the daily streak,
and which words a child found hard are saved **only on the phone**, so the
app can pick up where the child left off. They are not uploaded anywhere.
Uninstalling the app, or deleting the account from inside it, removes them.

**Lesson content.** The app downloads new lessons from GitHub, where they
are hosted. Like any website, GitHub receives the phone's IP address when
it does. Nothing about the child or the account is sent.

**Crash reports.** If the app crashes, it sends a report to Google's
Firebase Crashlytics service: what went wrong in the app's code, the
phone's model and Android version, the app version, and a random
identifier for this installation of the app (so repeat crashes on one
phone can be told apart from many phones crashing once). It does **not**
include the account email, the child's progress, or anything typed or
said. Reports are kept for 90 days. A grown-up can turn this off in the
app under **For grown-ups → Send crash reports**; reports not yet sent
are then deleted from the phone.

**Speech.** Peekadoo reads words aloud using the phone's own text-to-speech
engine. It chooses a voice that works offline wherever the phone has one.
If a phone only has an online voice, the lesson text being read (for
example "The apple is red.") is processed by that voice's provider, usually
Google. Nothing personal is ever spoken or sent.

## Children

Peekadoo is designed for children aged 3 and up, and for the grown-ups who
look after them.

- A child is never asked for any information — not a name, not an age, not
  a photo, not their voice.
- The account belongs to the grown-up who sets it up.
- There is no chat, no sharing, no messaging, and no way for a child to
  contact or be contacted by anyone.
- Anything to do with the account or with payments sits behind a
  grown-up check that a young child can't pass.

We don't knowingly collect personal information from children. If you
believe a child has given us personal information, contact us and we will
delete it.

## Subscriptions

If you subscribe to All Access, payment is handled entirely by
**Google Play** — we never see card or payment details. Subscriptions are
managed through **RevenueCat**, which tells the app whether a subscription
is active using an anonymous identifier, not your name or email. You can
cancel in the Google Play app under **Payments & subscriptions**.

## Who processes data for us

| Service | What for | What it receives |
|---|---|---|
| Google Firebase Authentication | Signing in | Email address, password (stored scrambled), IP address |
| Google Firebase Crashlytics | Crash reports (can be switched off) | What went wrong, phone model and Android version, app version, a random installation identifier |
| GitHub | Hosting lesson content | IP address |
| Google Play and RevenueCat | Subscriptions, if you subscribe | Purchase status, an anonymous identifier |

## How long we keep it

The account is kept until you delete it. When you do, it is deleted
straight away and not kept anywhere else. See the
[account deletion page](delete-account) for how.

## Your rights

You can ask us at any time what we hold about you, ask us to correct it,
or ask us to delete it. Email us at the address below and we will reply
within 30 days.

## Changes to this policy

significant — for example, if the app starts collecting something new —
